## What got done today

**The SEO audit**

- The "Core Themes" cards now go to the real category pages, and their counts match the essay list.
- The Four Doors cards and "Diagnose · Engage" are now real links Google can follow.
- Each essay shows the same date everywhere (some showed a day apart).
- Essays no longer repeat their own title as the first line.
- Google now gets better information about each essay: author, date, category and a breadcrumb trail.
- Photos have descriptions, and link previews use the right image size.
- Two categories with only one essay are hidden from Google until they have more.

**Speed**

- Main photos load first on 21 pages.
- Images are sized for the screen, saving up to 150 KB on some pages.
- About 115 KB of unneeded code no longer loads on the homepage and essays page.

**Matching the live site**

- 20 pages now match live's spacing and title sizes.
- Six pages that live recently redesigned now match it: Grant Study, Dharma Finder, Self-Portrait,
  Search, the Peptide quiz (with its picture) and SoulScore.
- Search now finds essays, pages, coffees and charities. Live's search shows no results at all.

**Problems found and fixed along the way**

- On the Grant Study quiz, the answer buttons could not be clicked. Fixed.
- The email step before quiz results ran off the side of phone screens on all 17 quizzes. Fixed.
- Some pages could be dragged sideways on phones. Fixed.

**Look and feel**

- Emoji and typed arrows used as icons are now proper icons across the site (about 360 replaced).
  Arrows inside Tony's own writing were left alone.

**Security**

- Added standard protections: other sites can't frame our pages, and visitors' privacy settings are respected.
- "You Are the Moat" is password-protected again. The essay text is only sent after the right
  password, and the password is not stored in the code.

**New member features (they need a login)**

- **Accounts:** sign up, sign in, forgot password.
- **My Highlights:** select a passage in any essay to save it, then find it later in "Your Commonplace Book".
- **My Impact:** each member gets an invite link and sees who joined through it.
- **Clock Keeper Part II:** the response form now saves answers. This also fixes a broken link from the Part I essay.
- **Post-Intervention check-in:** saves each anonymous check-in.
- **Three Friends Gate:** a seeker names three friends. Each friend gets an email, confirms it's
  them with a code, and answers six questions. Results unlock when all three answer.
- **PRI Research:** an admin-only page with the research data and a spreadsheet download. The PRI
  calibration quiz again asks "Contribute to Research?".
