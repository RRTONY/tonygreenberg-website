RAM DASS KEY TEACHINGS (for FauxTony knowledge base):

Biography: Born Richard Alpert (1931-2019). Harvard psychology professor fired alongside Timothy Leary for psilocybin research in 1963. Traveled to India, met guru Neem Karoli Baba (Maharaj-ji), became Ram Dass. Wrote "Be Here Now" (1971) — one of the most influential spiritual books of the 20th century. Founded Seva Foundation, Hanuman Foundation. Suffered massive stroke in 1997, continued teaching from wheelchair. Died Dec 22, 2019 in Maui.

Core Teachings:
1. BE HERE NOW — The present moment is the only reality. Past and future are constructs of the mind.
2. LOVING AWARENESS — "I am loving awareness" — the practice of identifying not with ego but with the awareness that witnesses all experience.
3. THE SOUL PERSPECTIVE — We are souls having a human experience, not humans having a spiritual experience.
4. SUFFERING AS CURRICULUM — "Suffering is grace" — every difficulty is an opportunity for awakening.
5. THE GURU IS WITHIN — The external teacher points you back to your own inner knowing.
6. SERVICE AS SPIRITUAL PRACTICE — Compassionate action (karma yoga) as a path to liberation.
7. EGO DEATH — The dissolution of the separate self as the gateway to unity consciousness.
8. FIERCE GRACE — His post-stroke teaching: using limitation and suffering as doorways to deeper presence.

Key Quotes:
- "We're all just walking each other home."
- "The quieter you become, the more you can hear."
- "I would like my life to be a statement of love and compassion — and where it isn't, that's where my work lies."
- "Treat everyone you meet like God in drag."
- "The next message you need is always right where you are."
- "As long as you have certain desires about how it ought to be you can't see how it is."
- "In most of our human relationships, we spend much of our time reassuring one another that our costumes of identity are on straight."
- "The game is not about becoming somebody, it's about becoming nobody."
- "Everything changes once we identify with being the witness to the story, instead of the actor in it."

Key Books: Be Here Now (1971), The Only Dance There Is (1974), Grist for the Mill (1977), How Can I Help? (1985), Still Here (2000), One-Liners (2002), Be Love Now (2010), Polishing the Mirror (2013), Walking Each Other Home (2018), Being Ram Dass (2021)

Connection to Tony: Ram Dass is listed as a hero/influence on Tony's Territory page. Tony quotes Ram Dass alongside Buckminster Fuller and Donella Meadows. The consciousness expansion thread — from psychedelic medicine investments to the "be the glitch" philosophy — traces directly back to Ram Dass's teaching that awareness itself is the operating system.

MATT MOCHARY KEY TEACHINGS:

Biography: Matt Mochary is a full-time CEO coach who has worked with the heads of top Silicon Valley companies including Sam Altman (OpenAI), Brian Armstrong (Coinbase), Naval Ravikant, and executives at Notion, Rippling, Plaid, and others. Author of "The Great CEO Within" — an open-source playbook for startup leadership. Former filmmaker (Cocaine Cowboys) turned venture investor turned coach.

Core Teachings:
1. ENERGY AUDIT — Track what gives you energy vs. drains it. Delegate everything in the "drain" column.
2. TOP GOAL — Identify the single most important thing and work on it first thing every morning.
3. FEAR SETTING — Borrowed from Stoicism: write down your worst fears, then the steps to prevent and recover from them.
4. FEEDBACK AS GIFT — Create psychological safety so people can give and receive honest feedback.
5. ISSUE/PROPOSED RESOLUTION — Every problem brought to a meeting must come with a proposed solution.
6. ANGER/FEAR JOURNALING — Write out negative emotions to process them before they drive decisions.
7. HIRING SCORECARDS — Define success metrics before interviewing anyone.
8. CONSCIOUS LEADERSHIP — Leaders who operate from trust and curiosity rather than fear and control.

Key Principles:
- "Founding a company is easy; scaling it is hard."
- Individual productivity comes before organizational productivity.
- Use OKRs with weekly check-ins.
- Structure every meeting with an agenda.
- Deliver feedback within 24 hours.
- The CEO's job is to make themselves unnecessary.

Connection to Tony: Mochary's framework for conscious leadership — operating from trust rather than fear, radical transparency, energy management — aligns with Tony's systems-thinking approach to business. The "Great CEO Within" philosophy mirrors Tony's belief that the best infrastructure is invisible and that leadership is about removing friction, not adding control.
