Share Button Analysis:

1. The inline ShareBar (line 143 in BlogPost.tsx) uses window.open() for twitter/linkedin/email
   and navigator.clipboard.writeText() for copy. These look correct.

2. The StickyShareBar (line 293 in ConversionMechanics.tsx) also uses window.open() and 
   navigator.clipboard.writeText(). Also looks correct.

3. The issue from the screenshot: The INLINE ShareBar buttons are visible (SHARE, X, in, mail, copy)
   but the user says they don't work.

Possible issues:
- The buttons might be covered by another element (z-index issue)
- The window.open() calls might be blocked by popup blockers on mobile Safari
- The URL being shared might be wrong (using window.location.href which on mobile would be the manus.space URL)

The screenshot also shows raw markdown rendering: "**URL:**" and a tonygreenberg.com link
appearing as plain text in the blog post body. This is a content rendering issue.
