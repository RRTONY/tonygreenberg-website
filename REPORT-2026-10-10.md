## What got done today

**The old Manus database is replaced by Supabase**

- Every table from the old site is copied into a private archive area in Supabase (75,089 rows).
  A full backup is also kept on the webmaster's Mac.
- The old site's real reader reactions, ratings, micro-commitment and 24 real quiz results now
  show on the new site. Test data from the old site was left out.
- The old database connection is removed from the project.
- The site's Supabase secret key was a damaged copy, so comments, reactions and member features
  were failing. The correct key is now in place, on the test site too.

**Quizzes**

- Every quiz now saves each finish with its result (for example "The Systems Architect") and a
  score where there is one. 27 quizzes, including a few that saved nothing on the old site.
- Members who sign in keep their quiz progress on every device (tested with two browsers).
- Fixed: the result was lost if someone left the results page within about 5 seconds.

**Old forms save again, and Tony gets an email for each one**

The seven forms the old site saved: PRI consent, PRI corrections, the Living Declaration, the
"Submit Your Story" form (Protecting Your Business and Alex Azzi), the Facilitator Index (full form
and quick intake), spam reports and BrewSoul coffee suggestions. If a save ever fails, the form
offers the old "email Tony" option, so nothing is lost.

**Email works**

- Resend is set up; tonygreenberg.com is verified. Emails come from
  "Only Time Buys Trust <trust@tonygreenberg.com>".
- A test email to web.master@ramprate.com was delivered.
- This turns on: friend-gate invites and codes, and Tony's emails for new comments, "Ask Tony"
  questions and the forms above.
