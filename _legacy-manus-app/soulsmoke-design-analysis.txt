SoulSmoke Mezcal Site — Design Analysis

BRAND NAME: SOULSMOKE — "The Miracle of Mezcal"
TAGLINE: "What enters the mouth must speak to the mind and stay with the heart."

COLOR PALETTE:
- Primary background: Very dark green-black (#0D0D0A or similar)
- Accent: Lime/chartreuse green (#C5FF00 or similar) for CTAs and highlights
- Secondary accent: Gold/amber (#C5A23C) for badges (EVI, EEI)
- Text: Warm off-white/cream for body text
- Headlines: Bright white with decorative serif font

TYPOGRAPHY:
- Headlines: Decorative serif with medieval/alchemical feel — very large, dramatic
- "SOULSMOKE" wordmark: Custom blackletter/uncial style, massive
- Body: Elegant serif for descriptions
- Labels/CTAs: Sans-serif or mono, uppercase, with letter-spacing

LAYOUT STRUCTURE:
1. Hero: Full-viewport dark background, massive wordmark, tagline in italic serif, CTA button
2. "Be the Glitch" section: Split layout — text left, atmospheric photo right (clay pot over fire with green smoke)
3. "Two Ways to Measure Magic" — EVI and EEI rating system cards on dark background
4. "Choose Your Adventure" — 5 clickable cards with photo backgrounds (US Hunt, Mexico Source, Scent-to-Agave Finder, Agave Encyclopedia, Ancestral Process)
5. Email signup section
6. Journey progress indicator at bottom

KEY DESIGN ELEMENTS:
- Ambient sound toggle ("Light fire")
- SoulSmoke/Aficionado mode toggle
- EN/ES language toggle
- Photo backgrounds with dark overlays on cards
- Atmospheric photography: fire, smoke, agave fields, copper stills
- Journey progress indicator (1 of 6: Home)
- Green glow/smoke effects
- Cards with hover animations

INTERACTIVE FEATURES:
- Scent-to-Agave Finder (interactive flavor decoder)
- Agave Encyclopedia (27 species)
- Scroll-driven ancestral process journey
- Two rating indices (EVI, EEI)
- Collection of 25 US bottles, 25 Mexico legends

VOICE/TONE:
- Irreverent, passionate, anti-mainstream
- "Zero interest in the ordinary, the predictable"
- "Bottles that give your palate an existential crisis"
- "Rupture in the matrix of mass-produced spirits"
- Deeply knowledgeable but never pretentious

MULTI-PAGE STRUCTURE:
- Home, The Evolution, The Agaves, The Process
- Plus: Explore dropdown, More dropdown
