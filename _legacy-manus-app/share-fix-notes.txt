Share button problem analysis:

1. StickyShareBar (ConversionMechanics.tsx line 293):
   - Desktop: fixed left sidebar at left:1rem, vertically centered - uses "hidden md:flex"
   - Mobile: fixed bottom bar - uses "flex md:hidden"
   
   PROBLEM: The desktop version at left:1rem overlaps blog content on medium screens.
   The "hidden md:flex" means it shows at md (768px+), but the blog content column 
   starts at the left edge on tablets. The left:1rem puts buttons right over text.

2. ShareBar (BlogPost.tsx line 143):
   - Inline share bar placed before the article content (line 656)
   - This is fine - it's static, not overlapping

FIX APPROACH:
- Remove the floating left-side desktop sticky entirely
- Keep the mobile bottom bar but make it more compact
- The inline ShareBar at top of article + the bottom-of-article share section are sufficient
- Add a share section at the END of the article too (after the CTA)
- This way: inline share at top, inline share at bottom, mobile bottom bar while scrolling
- Much cleaner, no overlap, unobtrusive

Alternative: Move desktop sticky to right side with more offset, but that still risks overlap.
Best: Kill the floating desktop sidebar, rely on inline shares + mobile bottom bar.
