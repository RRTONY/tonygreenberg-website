Public Health · Psychedelic Medicine

When Healing Becomes Extraction

The psychedelic renaissance is real. So is the industry forming around it. And some of what that industry is doing will kill people.

✦   ✦   ✦

There is a moment in every genuine healing tradition when someone decides to charge for it. That moment is not inherently corrupt. Teachers need to eat. Spaces need to be maintained. The question is what happens next — whether the practice remains in service of the person, or whether the person becomes in service of the practice.

"The waiver is not informed consent. It is the legal architecture of deniability."

Six laws. Not guidelines. Not suggestions. Laws — in the sense that violating them produces predictable harm, regardless of intention, regardless of credential, regardless of how beautiful the ceremony was.
