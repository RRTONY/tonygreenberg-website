# Surreal Jewel Popup Design Direction

Key constraints from user preferences:
- NO blue and gold combo (avoid)
- NO "classy" — needs to be edgy, quirky, rule-breaking
- Sacred geometry + Japanese minimalism blend
- High contrast, natural colors, multicolored
- Glitched finish, movement, dynamic
- NOT flat — needs 3D depth, layered glass, light refraction
- Surreal, magnificent, awe-dropping
- Makes someone EXCITED to come back or sign up

Design approach — "The Portal":
- Deep dark backdrop (not pure black — deep warm charcoal with aurora/nebula undertones)
- Multiple layered glass panels at different depths (CSS perspective + transforms)
- Animated aurora/nebula gradient shifting behind the glass — warm earth tones + emerald + amber + rose
- Floating luminous particles (like fireflies or dust in a cathedral beam)
- Sacred geometry wireframe rotating slowly behind content
- Prismatic light refraction on glass edges (rainbow edge highlights)
- The card itself feels like looking into a crystal — depth, light, movement
- Subtle glitch effect on the headline text
- Input field that glows with inner light when focused
- CTA button that pulses with living energy

Color palette (natural, high-contrast, NO blue+gold):
- Deep backdrop: #0D0B0A (warm near-black)
- Aurora colors: amber #C4841D, rose #B85A5A, emerald #3D8B6E, warm white #F5EDE0
- Glass: rgba(245,237,224,0.08) with backdrop-filter blur
- Particles: warm amber/rose/emerald dots
- Text: #F5EDE0 (warm cream) on dark
- Accents: prismatic edge highlights

The popup should feel like peering into a living jewel box — a portal to something rare.
